# proprioception

## In short

The structural faculty: a system's sense of what it is made of and how it is wired together.

## What it is

Proprioception is the structural faculty of self-knowledge: the system's sense of its own composition — which parts exist, what each is for, which layer it belongs to, and what depends on what. It is carried by the index every part must enter in order to exist, the verifier that compares that index against the code, the map of dependencies, the layer rules, and the relationships recorded in the build's machine-readable output.

## Why it exists

It names the sense most of the platform serves today and keeps it separate from the other two, so that a claim about structure is not read as a claim about behaviour. A system with proprioception can answer what would break if this changed without anybody reading the code.

## When to use it

Use proprioception when the sentence is about structure: what exists, what it is called, where it sits, and what it depends on.

## When not to use it

Do not use proprioception for how a system is behaving while it runs — that is interoception — and do not use it as another word for the whole of self-knowledge. It carried that larger meaning before the three faculties were named separately, and it no longer does.
