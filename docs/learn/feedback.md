# feedback

This page is generated. Every line below is read from s0ma itself when the documentation is built, so editing it here would be undone by the next build — the place to make a change is the thing being described.

## In short

A report to the people who make s0ma, shown to you in full and sent only after you approve that exact report.

## What it is

Feedback is the path for telling the people who make s0ma what happened: a report prepared on your own machine, shown to you whole, and sent only after you have approved that exact report. Preparation is local and needs no network. The feedback data is redacted by a fixed rule and limited to an agreed list of context, so no transcripts, prompts, source, raw logs, or hardware identifiers travel by default. What you approved is what is transmitted, what is stored, and what the receipt refers back to.

## Why it exists

Sending anything from a developer's machine is a trust decision, and the only honest way to ask for it is to show the whole feedback report first and let the person say no. Building it this way also keeps it harmless: nothing about preparing or sending a feedback report can change what a build, a check, or a running system does.

## When to use it

Use the feedback path when something about s0ma itself is worth reporting — a diagnostic that confused you, a step that failed, a guide that misled you — and expect to read the report before it goes.

## When not to use it

Do not describe a feedback report as anonymous; it is pseudonymous and locally redacted, which is a different and more honest claim. Do not treat feedback as background collection either — nothing is gathered while you work, and nothing leaves the machine without a person approving that exact report.
