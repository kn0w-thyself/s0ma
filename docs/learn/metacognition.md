## In short

The reasoning faculty: answering questions by working over the system's own model rather than over its code.

## What it is

Metacognition is the reasoning faculty of self-knowledge: answering questions about the system by reasoning over its recorded model instead of re-reading its source. Asking what would break if this changed, asking where a new part belongs, asking a question in plain words, and noticing that the model and the code no longer agree are all metacognition. Each takes the recorded model as its subject, and each follows fixed rules rather than calling a language model, so the answers are reproducible and available offline.

## Why it exists

It is what makes the record worth keeping. A record nobody can question is an archive; a record that answers what would break if this changed, and where a new part belongs, is the reason the other two faculties are maintained at all.

## When to use it

Use metacognition when the sentence is about reasoning over the recorded model: queries, impact analysis, guidance about where something belongs, and the detection of disagreement between the model and reality.

## When not to use it

Do not use metacognition for the act of recording structure or measuring behaviour — those are the other two faculties, and this is what is done with what they record. Do not let it imply a language model is involved: the reasoning here is rule-based by principle, so that a system can answer for itself with no network and no model call.
