# Code of conduct

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**. The full text is
the authority, and it is published here:

<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>

## What that means here, in our own words

Take part in a way that would make somebody new want to stay. Assume the person
you are replying to is trying, ask before you correct, and let people be wrong in
public without paying for it. Disagreement is welcome and expected — it is how
software gets better — and it stays about the work rather than about the person.

What is not tolerated: harassment of any kind, sexual attention or advances,
insults and personal attacks, publishing somebody's private information without
their permission, and sustained disruption of a conversation. Nor is the quieter
version of the same thing — the reply written to make somebody feel small for
not knowing something yet.

This applies wherever the project is: issues, discussions, pull requests, commit
messages, and anywhere somebody is representing s0ma in public.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks this, write to
**conduct@kn0wthyself.ai**.

You do not need to be sure it counts, and you do not need evidence to hand — a
sentence saying what happened is enough to start with.

## What happens next

- **We acknowledge it privately**, to you, and nowhere else.
- **The maintainer handles it.** This is a one-person project today, so there is
  no committee and no appeal to a wider body; if your report is about the
  maintainer, say so and we will bring in somebody independent.
- **We keep it confidential.** We do not share who reported what — not with the
  person reported, not publicly, and not in a changelog.
- **We tell you what we decided.** Depending on what happened that can be a
  private word, a warning, having something removed, or being blocked from the
  project.

Reporting something in good faith never counts against you, whatever the outcome.
